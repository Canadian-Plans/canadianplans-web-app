## 4. Reply set for common questions

| Question                          | Reply                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Do I pay on the website?          | No. Nothing is paid online. Once your plan is active, the provider bills you directly.                                                                                            |
| Who can get the pre-arrival eSIM? | Monthly Rogers and Fido plans are for study permit, work permit and permanent resident arrivals staying over 90 days. Chatr prepaid plans are for visitors staying under 90 days. |
| What documents do I need?         | Mobile plans need a government ID and an identity and credit check. Pre-arrival eSIM and internet orders upload nothing; an advisor asks if anything is needed.                   |
| How fast is it?                   | An advisor confirms your order, usually the same day. Most home internet installs happen within about 3 days.                                                                     |
| Is internet available in Ontario? | Not yet. Join the wish list on the order page and we'll email you when Ontario opens.                                                                                             |
| Can I cancel?                     | Yes, free of charge any time before the plan is activated. After activation you cancel with the provider under its terms.                                                         |
| Can I keep my number?             | Yes. Keep your current plan active and give us your account number and PIN; we move it for you.                                                                                   |
