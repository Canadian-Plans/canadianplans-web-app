**[OWNER TO CONFIRM: legal business name]** ("Canadian Plans", "we", "us") respects your
privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our
website, place an order or submit an enquiry, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the
choices available to you.

We handle personal information in line with Canada's _Personal Information Protection and
Electronic Documents Act_ (PIPEDA), Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) and applicable
provincial privacy legislation.

## 1. Information we collect

When you submit a form on our website, we ask for:

- **Contact details** — your full name, email address and phone number.
- **Address** — street address, unit number, city and postal code, and the province you
  choose. For internet and TV, availability and pricing depend on your exact location.
- **Date of birth** (internet and TV) — used to verify identity and confirm eligibility with
  the providers we work with.
- **Service choices** — the plan you selected and, depending on the plan, your preferred
  installation time, your current provider, whether you want to keep your number, the
  number of lines, and eSIM or physical SIM.
- **Identity document** (mobile plans) — one copy of government-issued ID (driver's
  licence, provincial ID or passport), the type of ID, and your consent to use it for a
  credit check.
- **Documents for your plan** — any other documents a plan requires.
- **Wish list** — if we do not yet serve your province, your email address and the plans
  you are interested in, if you choose to leave them.

We also record the date, time and version of the terms and consents you accepted, so we can
demonstrate the basis on which we contacted you.

**Information collected automatically.** Our website may collect standard technical
information such as IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. We use privacy-
friendly analytics that count visits and the provinces and plans people look at, without
recording personal details. We use a bot-protection check on forms.

## 2. Why we collect it

We use your personal information only to:

- contact you about the order or enquiry you submitted;
- check availability and pricing at your address, and, for mobile plans, carry out the
  identity and credit check;
- arrange installation, activation or delivery of your service if you choose to proceed;
- decide which provinces and plans to add next; and
- meet our legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing
without your consent.

## 3. Consent

By submitting a form you consent to being contacted by phone, text or email about the
services you asked about, and to our using the information you provided for the purposes
above. Marketing messages are sent only if you separately opt in.

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: privacy
contact email]**. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer process your order.

## 5. How we protect and keep it

Records are stored in an access-controlled database. Access is limited to staff who need it
to do their job, and information is transmitted over an encrypted connection. Identity
documents are stored privately and are available only through short-lived, permission-
checked links.

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or
as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

## 6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

- ask what personal information we hold about you;
- ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- ask us to delete your information, where we are not required to keep it; and
- withdraw your consent to being contacted.

Contact us at **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: privacy contact email]** and we will respond within a
reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of
